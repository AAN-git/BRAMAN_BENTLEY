Protection

PermaPlate Protection

There's nothing more beautiful than a high-luster paint finish, especially when it enhances the sleek lines and striking presence of a luxury vehicle like a Bentley. Achieving that showroom-quality shine is one thing, but maintaining it requires superior protection against the elements. That’s why every Bentley vehicle from Bentley Palm Beach receives the added benefit of PermaPlate protection — a premium-grade polymer coating designed to preserve your vehicle's flawless finish. PermaPlate's advanced formula creates an invisible yet highly durable barrier that actively defends against harmful environmental factors. The coating effectively blocks the sun's intense ultraviolet rays, which are known to cause paint fading, discoloration, and oxidation over time. By preventing this damage, PermaPlate ensures your Bentley's rich, deep paint color remains vibrant and eye-catching. In addition to UV protection, this powerful polymer shield also resists damage from industrial fallout, surface rust, and airborne contaminants that can compromise your vehicle's finish.

PermaPlate not only protects your paint but also enhances its glossy shine, allowing your Bentley to stand out wherever you go. It simplifies routine cleaning, making it easier to maintain that showroom-worthy appearance with minimal effort. With PermaPlate, you can drive confidently knowing your vehicle's exquisite finish is safeguarded for years to come.

Tires

Nitro-Filled Tires

At Bentley Palm Beach, we believe that every detail of your Bentley should reflect the brand’s dedication to excellence, including the care of its tires. That’s why we offer NitroFill, a high-purity nitrogen gas, to optimize your vehicle’s tire performance and longevity. Unlike regular air, which contains moisture and oxygen, NitroFill eliminates these elements, reducing the risk of corrosion and pressure fluctuations within the tire. This helps maintain consistent tire pressure, which not only extends tire life but also improves fuel efficiency, driving comfort, and handling. The reduction in moisture and oxygen also slows down the rate of tire wear, ensuring that your Bentley’s tires perform at their best for longer periods. By stabilizing tire pressure over time, NitroFill reduces the likelihood of blowouts, making your driving experience safer. In addition, with better tire performance, your vehicle’s overall handling and ride quality are enhanced, maintaining the smooth, precise experience Bentley drivers expect.

At Bentley Palm Beach, we’re committed to offering you the highest quality service and care for your luxury vehicle. Investing in NitroFill is a simple yet effective way to protect your tires, optimize performance, and keep your Bentley driving at its best for years to come.
