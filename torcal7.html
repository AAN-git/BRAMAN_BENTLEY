The Range

A Choice of Two Groundbreaking Models

Together, the Torcal and the performance-led Torcal S mark the beginning of a new era, not just in performance, but in craftsmanship and the way that technology can be used to optimise the travelling experience.

Sleek and minimalist in design, both Torcal models have incredible road presence – with the floating diamond grille using 76 hand-applied LED diamonds to stunning effect. Inside, ergonomic curved screens meet handcrafted veneers, amid upholstery crafted from sumptuous leather and sustainable, suede-like Dinamica, while the Bentley Dynamic Symphony uses the sound of real musical instruments to take the raw emotion of a V8 exhaust note into an exciting new era.

Exterior

Designed for the Road Ahead

The arrival of the Torcal marks the debut of a sleeker, more progressive, more minimalist Bentley design language – one inspired by the brand’s heritage yet crafted for the road ahead.

An evolving design DNA

As the first execution of Bentley’s progressive new design vision, the Torcal’s silhouette is clean and minimalist, with satin silver exterior detailing, for a subtle, contemporary aesthetic.

Muscular haunches and tailgate

The tailgate design is inspired by the luggage carrier of the historic ‘Blue Train’ Bentley. Below it, a gloss black ‘horseshoe’ emphasises the width of the vehicle, complementing its muscular haunches.

Interior

A Cabin Crafted Like No Other

To enhance the travelling experience for all occupants, the interior of the Torcal brings together traditional craft techniques and natural materials with truly innovative new uses of light and space.

Hand-finished hides

The cabin features a sleek, single-colour upholstery pattern, comprising handcrafted leather for the lower parts of the cabin and suede-like Dinamica for the upper areas.

Advanced seat comfort

The front seats feature 16-way electrical adjustment, alongside heating, ventilation and massage functions, while the bench seat in the rear offers powered adjustment as well as heating for the two outer seat positions, with ventilation also available as an option.

Exquisite veneers and switchgear

Handcrafted Ombre Walnut by Mulliner veneers add warmth and sophistication, while the satin finish of the switchgear and metal trim imbues the entire space with contemporary style.

New Bullseye Vector Sphere air vents

Inspired by Bentley’s classic Bullseye air vents, the new, 360-degree Bullseye Vector Sphere air vents deliver the sensation of a continuous ring of air, using air streams that converge into a single vector, for more accurate direction, a more natural sensation and quieter operation.

An incredible curved touchscreen

Taking pride of place in the heart of the fascia is a beautiful, vertically curved, 12.25” HD touchscreen. The first OLED screen to be fitted in a Bentley, it offers sharp, fast visuals, with vivid colour and true blacks, even when viewing the screen in daylight.

Performance

A New Age of Exhilaration Starts Here

Two powerful, high-performance electric motors combine to deliver a full 809 bhp (821 PS, 604 kW) and 1,155 Nm (852 lb ft) of torque. This translates into acceleration from zero to 60 mph in just 3.3 seconds (0-100 km/h in 3.4 seconds)*.

As well as all-wheel drive, the Torcal features four-wheel steering. This means that, at low speeds, the rear wheels steer in the opposite direction to the front ones at an angle of up to five degrees, reducing the car’s turning circle to just 11m. At higher speeds, rear wheels steer in the same direction as the front ones, for effortless manoeuvring and smoother lane changes.
