The Range

An Icon of the Open Road

Few cars turn heads like the Continental GT. Bentley’s icon for more than 20 years, its fourth generation is available in a choice of five stunning models.

The wondrous Continental GT Mulliner represents the pinnacle of grand touring performance and luxury, while the Continental GT Speed offers the same phenomenal power with unique sports styling. The Continental GT S, meanwhile, combines the best of Bentley’s chassis technology with a unique, sports-inspired attitude. The Continental GT Azure offers technology and craftsmanship designed for enhanced wellbeing, while the Continental GT offers the essence of the luxury grand tourer: thrilling performance balanced with exquisite comfort.

Exterior

A New Era of Grand Tourer Design

The Continental GT ushers in a new era of Bentley design. A new aerodynamic face houses innovative headlamps with an intense, steely stare, unlike that of any Continental before it.

Iconic black matrix grille

The iconic Bentley matrix grille is finished in a subtle black, while the surround and centre-bar are rendered in a contrasting bright chrome.

Subtle black front detailing

A gloss black front splitter combines with black lower grilles to complement the black matrix grille.

Bright chrome detailing

The sleek lines of the Continental GT are enhanced with eye-catching chrome details, from the front to the rear of the car.

Interior

Natural Finishes, Crafted by Hand

Handcrafted, natural materials are on display throughout, from the Nappa leather on the fluted seats to the leather-trimmed gear lever and heated steering wheel.

A choice of five hide colours

The leather upholstery is crafted by hand, with a choice of five stunning standard hide colours.

Dinamica detailing

Sections of Dinamica have been applied to the pillars between the windows and the headliner on the inside of the roof, adding a touch of sporting style to the entire upper cabin.

Rich Crown Cut Walnut veneers

For the hard surfaces on the dash, doors and centre console, rich Crown Cut Walnut veneers are fitted as standard.

Three sound systems to choose from

Even the audio system gives you three options to consider, with the Bentley Signature Audio system installed as standard, and both the Bang & Olufsen for Bentley system and the Naim for Bentley system available as optional upgrades.

Performance

Power Has Never Felt This Good

Beneath the bonnet of the Continental GT is the High Performance Hybrid powertrain – a 4.0 litre, turbocharged V8 engine coupled with an intelligent electric motor, for smooth, effortless acceleration. Offering a compelling blend of refinement and power, it deploys the electric motor and petrol engine to deliver a full 930 Nm of torque. The result? Acceleration from 0-60 mph in just 3.5 seconds and a top track speed of 168 mph.
