New Bentayga Speed

An Engine Like No Other

Featuring a phenomenal twin-turbocharged 4.0 litre V8 engine, now tuned to deliver a full 850 Nm of torque and 650 PS, the new Bentayga Speed is the most powerful Bentayga that Bentley has ever built. A showcase of sporting luxury and exhilarating performance, it is a vehicle without equal.

As one of the last production Bentleys to be fitted with a pure V8 engine before the company completes its transition to hybrid and electric propulsion, the Bentayga Speed offers a unique opportunity for those who appreciate the power and the unmistakable roar of a V8 unleashed. This is the fastest and most powerful Bentayga ever produced.

Unmistakable Exterior Details

A dominant presence on the road, the new Bentayga Speed features dark tint exterior detailing across its sleek bodywork, from its imposing matrix front grille to its oval tailpipe finishers at the rear.

In addition to seven standard exterior colours, there is an extended range of optional finishes that includes new satin colours, a range of duo-tone options and the revived Mulliner Legacy Colours range. Introduced with the launch of Bentayga Atelier Edition, this range makes five beautiful heritage colours easily accessible once again. Taken together, these paint ranges give you around 80 finishes to choose from.

A wide range of wheels are available, in sizes of up to 23”, all of which can be complemented with a custom brake calliper colour. The range includes shades from the classic sports red to subtle, silvery Ice, as well as more striking choices including Mandarin, Hyper Green, Signal Yellow and Klein Blue.

Exquisite Interior Design

The entire cabin is clad in handcrafted hides available in a wide range of colours, featuring a contemporary new Precision Diamond Quilting pattern, created specifically for Bentley’s Speed models.

Inside the cabin, you can choose from Bentley’s entire range of 15 primary hide colours, with the unique Speed colour layout offering further opportunities for personalisation, including accent colours, contrast stitching and contrast piping.
