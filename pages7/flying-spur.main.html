Exterior

Designed to Turn Heads

With a striking new look, the new Flying Spur’s exterior evokes both its phenomenal performance and the luxury to be experienced inside.

Matt black matrix grille

The iconic Bentley matrix grille is finished in a subtle matt black, complemented by a chrome surround and centre bar.

Black lower grilles

To match the main matrix grille, the lower bumper grilles are also finished in matt black, while a gloss black front splitter injects the car with even more presence.

Bright chrome exterior detailing

Aside from the front grilles and splitter, the majority of the exterior detailing is rendered in luxurious bright chrome.

Interior

A Range of Hide Colours

The interior is upholstered with hand-finished, fluted Nappa leather, in your choice of five colours.

Dinamica details

To complement the Nappa leather, tactile Dinamica has been used on the roof lining and the headrest cushions in the rear.

Ergonomic seating

The Front & Rear Comfort Specification offers a six-setting massage function in all four seats, plus electrical adjustment of the headrests, cushions and backrest bolsters.

Control for rear-seat passengers

Passengers in the rear benefit from the Touchscreen Remote (TSR), a removable wireless tablet that offers access to virtually all the controls available to the front seat passenger. That includes the audio system, the air conditioning system and the heating, ventilation and massage functions in the seats. If the Wellness Seating option is selected, the TSR also provides access to Postural Adjust** and Seat Auto Climate, two truly innovative features that can help you feel even more comfortable, especially on long journeys.

Bright or dark chrome detailing

The switchgear and other metal details are finished in bright chrome as standard, however you can opt to add the Dark Chrome Interior Specification, for a more sport-inspired look.

** Availability of features, options, and specifications may vary by model, and not all items shown may be available on every vehicle as standard.

Performance

Smooth, Surging Power

The Bentley High Performance Hybrid powertrain couples a turbocharged 4.0 litre V8 petrol engine with an advanced electric motor, to deliver a range of driving modes. With both the engine and the motor engaged, the car can accelerate from 0-60 mph in just 3.8 seconds (0-100 km/h in 3.9 seconds) and reach a top speed of 168 mph (270 km/h). It can also be driven in electric-only mode, for a range of up to 50 miles (80 km).

All-wheel steering is a standard feature, shrinking the car’s turning circle at low speeds, while also allowing for smoother lane changes and overtaking when cruising on the highway. Twin valve dampers allow for a greater difference in character between driving modes, while the Bentley Dynamic Ride anti-roll system reduces body movement, especially when cornering.
