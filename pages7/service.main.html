Mike Richmond · Service Manager

Mike was born and raised in northern New Jersey before relocating to Florida in 2018. Mike spent the first half of his career as a Porsche technician, and spent the second half as a Bentley Master Technician and Rolls-Royce technician/Shop Foreman. He was promoted to Service Manager in 2013, and his team was awarded with Bentley Elite Club status 3 out of 5 years. Mike joined Braman Motorcars as Service Manager of Bentley and Rolls-Royce in March of ’18.
