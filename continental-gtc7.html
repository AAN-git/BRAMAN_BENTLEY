The Range

Unprecedented Open-Top Style

Impossibly stylish with the top up or down, the Continental GTC represents the latest generation of the ultimate convertible grand tourer.

For those who demand the best of all convertible worlds, the Continental GTC Mulliner represents the pinnacle of open-top grand touring. The phenomenally powerful Continental GTC Speed, meanwhile, takes performance and sporting luxury to unprecedented levels. The new Continental GTC S combines truly exhilarating handling with bold, sports-inspired details, while the Continental GTC Azure offers elevated comfort enhancing technology, while the Continental GTC offers a perfect balance of thrilling performance and convertible luxury.

Exterior

New Heights of Style

The Continental GTC has been redesigned to take cabriolet style even further – whether you’re travelling with the roof up or down.

Sleek black matrix grille

At the front of the Continental GTC, a black matrix grille takes pride of place, enhanced by a chrome surround and centre bar.

A four-layer soft top

With its four-layer soft top sealed shut, you will be every bit as insulated as you would be in the Continental GT coupé. Yet when the roof drops, it stows away neatly, allowing a single beautiful design line to sweep around the rear seats, as you enjoy driving in the open air.

Interior

Choice of Hide Colours

The Nappa leather on the handcrafted, fluted seats can be commissioned in your choice of five hide colours.

Wide range of veneers

Crown Cut Walnut veneers are fitted as standard, with a range of optional alternatives including high-gloss and open-pore woods, ‘piano’ gloss paint colours and technical finishes such as carbon fibre.

Enhanced with Iliada

Hand-finished Iliada fabric lines the four-layer roof.

Raise the roof

Unlike many convertibles, the Continental GTC enables you to raise or drop the roof while driving at speeds of up to 30 mph (50 km/h) – so if the weather changes you can react immediately, without stopping the car.

Cooling off

The driver’s seat and front passenger seat include cooling functions, to keep backs and legs comfortable, even on the hottest afternoons.

Warming up

For colder days, there are heating functions in the steering wheel and front armrest. In the front seats, neck warmers gently circulate warm air around your shoulders.

Performance

Power Meets Refinement

By combining a turbocharged V8 petrol engine with an advanced electric motor, the Continental GTC can deliver phenomenal power when you need it, alongside a smooth, refined drive when cruising through city streets. Its High Performance Hybrid powertrain generates 930 Nm of torque when operating at full power, resulting in acceleration from 0-60 mph in just 3.8 seconds and a maximum track speed of 168 mph.

You can drive it on electric power alone and reach a max track speed of up to 87 mph (140 km/h) without engaging the V8 engine.

Personalisation

Create Your Perfect Convertible

Your options for personalisation begin with the exterior finish. There is a choice of seven standard colours, however when you add the extended range of paints and the collection of premium finishes available from Mulliner, your total number of options rises to around 80 colours.

Next comes the four-layer fabric roof, which can be commissioned in your choice of black, blue or grey – whichever complements your choice of paintwork most effectively.
