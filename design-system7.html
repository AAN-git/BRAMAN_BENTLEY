Every element of the site, on the site's own stylesheets: what you see here is the real component, not a drawing of it. Values are read from css/tokens.css; the theme is Satin Linen 40% (<html class="theme-satin">).

Vehicle cards

One card everywhere: photograph, name, facts, and the price plate — Final price leads; MSRP and the two dealer charges sit beneath as the working. A lease offer carries its term and the money down (Bentley's word: "down").

Flags under the photograph

Never on the photograph — nothing may cover the car (the client's Bentley note). One flag at a time, as a band across the card directly under its photograph. The band's room is kept only in a row where a card carries a flag: the other cards in that row keep the same empty room, so the photographs, names and prices stay level across the row; a row with no flag has no band and no empty room. A row of lease flags (three lines) is taller than a row of one-line marks, and only that row. Built with CSS subgrid (each card spans three rows: photograph, band, body), so the browser levels each row on its own; on the one-row home rail, :has() keeps the room only when the rail holds a flag. Certified Pre-Owned is a band mark of its own (the page's ground, --day, black type, a hairline above), for a certified car with no other flag; a certified car with a lease keeps "Certified by Bentley" in its kind line, one flag at a time. The first line is the mark in capitals; a lease's terms and condition follow in their own case. Lease flags are Heritage White with black type; Sale Pending is Bentley Green, Sold Dark Grey, both with white type. On the car page the offer carries its own flag, Lease special (see Car page).

Rules

Price (FTC 2026 · Florida § 501.976)

  • The figure that stands out is the Final price: the sale price with the dealer's charges in it ($1,189 dealer service + $514 electronic filing). Only tax, tag, registration and title may be left out.
  • MSRP and "+ Dealer service charge", "+ Electronic filing charge" sit beneath as the working.
  • "You save" is measured against the Final price, never the pre-fee price.
  • Every lease payment carries its term and the money down beside it, in Bentley's word: down.
  • A lease that rests on a rebate says so beside the payment, with the credit it needs.
  • Sold cars are not advertised: they leave the listing, its count, filters and the home rail. A pending deal reads Sale Pending.

Photographs

  • The whole motor car is always in the frame: move the frame, never crop the car.
  • Words never stand on the car — on the road, the ground, the sky, or a plate.
  • Photographs only; no video (dead cell-service pockets make a film look broken).
  • Cars have air around them; frames in a row are equal.

Forms (approved)

  • The label stands on the field's line, where the value will go — Body Light, Dark Grey 40%, in its own case — and rises above the line at 14px once the field has focus or a value, as bentleymotors.com does it. Approved by the client's side (Alex, 2026-10-26: "a good move"). Every form on the site, every new one.
  • Two columns; one on a phone. A 1px line in the ink, 2px with focus, no box round a field.
  • A list ends in a firm short chevron in the ink; until something is chosen it shows only its label.
  • The enquiry: address, form, hours (Alex, 2026-10-26). The note Fields marked with (*) are required. is the form's first line, inside its plate, across both columns. Beside the form, the column holds the heading, Our Location, the department's phone, Get directions / Send to phone, and under them that department's hours (Parts Hours on Parts; Sales Hours on Finance and the model pages) in tighter record rows. From 1024px the column is as tall as the form: the heading's capitals on the plate's top edge, the hours' last rule on its bottom edge (.enquire--fit). Contact and About keep their three-department Opening Hours chapter; Sell keeps the client's order (form, then hours and location under it).
  • Dropdowns: the closed field is the browser's own <select> on the field's ruled line — the listing's filters (Condition, Model, Year, Sort by) and every form. The open list is the site's own, as the demo site draws it (Alex, 2026-10-26; js/page7.js, .dd in static7.css): it drops from the field's line (8px under it), its left edge on the field's, at least the field's width; a white plate with a hairline (--rule), no shadow; one choice to a 40px row, 16px, 16px side padding; model names in capitals; on the listing's picks the number of cars each choice leaves (/8, Dark Grey, 14px, at the right — counted with the other picks as they stand); the chosen row marked by a 2px line at its left, the row under the pointer or the keys on Satin Linen; its height is its choices — never the demo's empty foot; past ~10 choices it scrolls with a thin bar.
