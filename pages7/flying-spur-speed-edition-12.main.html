Speed Edition 12

Celebrate the End of an Era

The Flying Spur Speed Edition 12 is part of a limited series of vehicles created to mark the end of a glorious era in automotive history.

As one of the last vehicles to be fitted with the iconic 6.0 litre Bentley W12 engine, it delivers the phenomenal power and technology that defines the Flying Spur Speed, alongside a range of handcrafted commemorative features that include a representation of the engine’s cylinder firing sequence, displayed on the fascia. Every car is also supplied with a scale model of the Bentley W12 engine, cast from a genuine recycled W12 engine block and housed in an exquisite presentation box.

The W12

The Power Behind the Brand

The engine in the Flying Spur Speed Edition 12 is a Bentley legend in itself. It was first seen in the original Continental GT, launched in 2003. Since then, it has become an important part of the brand’s DNA, holding great significance for anyone who has ever driven or admired a modern Bentley.

The company’s founder, W.O. Bentley, had always believed that the way to build more powerful, faster cars was to build bigger, better-designed engines. It was this philosophy that drove the introduction of more W12-powered modern Bentleys, among them the Flying Spur. In fact, for the last two decades, the W12 engine has been used constantly in Bentley’s most driver-focused models. Without it, cars like the Flying Spur Speed would never have been able to set such groundbreaking performance benchmarks.

Bentley remains the sole producer of W12-powered production cars – and every single engine, for every single car, is assembled by hand at the Bentley factory in Crewe, England.

Only 120 examples

Only 120 examples of the Flying Spur Speed Edition 12 sedan will ever be built. For those who prefer a different body style, limited numbers of the following vehicles are available: the Continental GT Speed Edition 12, the Continental GTC Speed Edition 12 and the Bentayga Speed Edition 12.

Performance

Unrivalled W12 Performance

The Flying Spur Speed Edition 12 is one of the fastest and most powerful four-door sedans on the planet – making it a fitting tribute to its incredible engine. Its top speed of 207 mph could only be achieved with an engine that can deliver 626 bhp and 900 Nm of torque, propelling the car from zero to 60 mph in just 3.7 seconds. With all this power comes responsibility, however – in the form of variable displacement. This feature allows six of the twelve cylinders to shut off automatically when driving at low speeds, increasing fuel efficiency and reducing emissions.

Despite the legendary status of the engine, there is even more to this car’s performance. As well as adaptive all-wheel drive, it features all-wheel steering, for a tighter turning circle at low speeds and smoother, more confident overtaking on the open road. Bentley Dynamic Ride – Bentley’s groundbreaking anti-roll technology – keeps the body stable at all times, allowing for greater control through corners, without compromising on comfort when cruising. The exhaust system, meanwhile, features Adaptive Valve Control, for a refined murmur when driving in Comfort mode and a more thrilling roar in Sport mode. Like every W12-powered Bentley, this is a car that puts incredible power at your disposal. How and when you use it is up to you.
