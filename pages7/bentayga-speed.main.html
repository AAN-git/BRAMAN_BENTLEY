Bentayga Speed

Unprecedented V8 Performance

Featuring a phenomenal twin-turbocharged 4.0 litre V8 engine, now tuned to deliver a full 850 Nm of torque and 650 PS, the Bentayga Speed is the most powerful Bentayga that Bentley has ever built. A showcase of sporting luxury and exhilarating performance, it is a vehicle without equal.

As one of the last production Bentleys to be fitted with a pure V8 engine before the company completes its transition to hybrid and electric propulsion, the Bentayga Speed offers a unique opportunity for those who appreciate the power and the unmistakable roar of a V8 unleashed. This is the fastest and most powerful Bentayga ever produced.

Exterior

This Is What Power Looks Like

A dominant presence on the road, the Bentayga Speed features dark tint exterior detailing across its sleek bodywork, from its imposing matrix front grille to its oval tailpipe finishers at the rear.

Akrapovic Titanium Sports Exhaust

With a sports exhaust fitted as standard, the Bentayga Speed sounds truly incredible. Add the optional titanium Akrapovic Sports Exhaust system, however, and you will have the loudest and most muscular sounding Bentayga model ever. Enhancing both the volume and the character of the car’s rich V8 exhaust note, its effects can be experienced both inside and outside the cabin. All you need to do is switch to Sport Mode and put your foot down.

Bentayga Styling Specification

The kit consists of a front splitter, rear diffuser, boot lip spoiler, and side sills branded with Bentley wings and has been designed to support the car's aerodynamic performance. If you choose to add the carbon fibre version, you can add a pinstripe in a colour that matches your choice of brake callipers.

Interior

Precision Diamond Quilting

The entire cabin is wrapped in meticulously handcrafted hides, offered in a broad palette of colours, and showcases an all-new Precision Diamond Quilting pattern—exclusively designed for Bentley’s Speed models.

Speed Colour Split

A new colour layout has been created for the Bentayga Speed, combining your choice of primary hide colour with eye-catching accent sections finished in Dinamica, where you and your passengers will benefit from the extra grip afforded by its tactile texture.

Speed Emblems

The prestigious ‘Speed’ emblem can be seen in numerous places in the car, from the badges on the fascia to the embroidery on the upright sections of the seats – making sure that all occupants are aware of your car’s identity.

Speed Graphics

Like all Bentleys, the Bentayga Speed offers a choice of styles for the graphics in the driver’s information panel behind the steering wheel, however it also features an option designed specifically for the Speed models.

Performance

Engineered for Exhilaration

Tuned specifically for the elite performance requirements of this Bentley model, the twin-turbocharged, 4.0 litre V8 engine delivers 641 bhp (650 PS) and 850 Nm (627 lb-ft) of torque – which translates directly into a 0-60 mph time of just 3.4 seconds – an incredible figure for a vehicle as spacious and luxurious as this. Maximum speed is equally exciting, at a full 193 mph (310 km/h)*.

In tandem with the biggest wheels ever fitted to a production Bentley are the biggest brakes. Choose the 23” wheels and you will also gain Carbon Ceramic Brakes, which feature 10 pistons on each of the front callipers, helping to deliver incredibly responsive stopping performance.

Personalisation

Take Things Even Further

In addition to seven standard exterior colours, there is an extended range of optional finishes that includes new satin colours, a range of duo-tone options and the revived Mulliner Legacy Colours range. Taken together, these paint ranges give you around 80 finishes to choose from.

Inside the cabin, you can choose from Bentley’s entire range of 15 primary hide colours, with the unique Speed colour layout offering further opportunities for personalisation, including accent colours, contrast stitching and contrast piping.
