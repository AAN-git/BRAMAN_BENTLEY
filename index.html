About Us

An official Bentley retailer
in West Palm Beach

Bentley Palm Beach represents Bentley Motors in West Palm Beach, Florida, and serves owners across Palm Beach County, the Treasure Coast and South Florida.

The showroom

The showroom presents the current range — Continental GT, Continental GT Convertible, Flying Spur and Bentayga — alongside a selection of Bentley Approved pre-owned motor cars. One retailer carries the whole of it: exploration, specification, handover and aftercare, with the same people on the file throughout.

New Bentley motor cars

Each new Bentley is built to the specification of its owner at the factory in Crewe, England. Our specialists take you through the commissioning process, from exterior paint and hide colour to veneer and Mulliner detailing, and keep you informed at every stage through to handover.

Bentley Approved pre-owned

Bentley Approved motor cars are prepared to factory standard by Bentley-trained technicians and supplied with a comprehensive warranty and roadside assistance. Every car is inspected against a detailed Bentley checklist before it is presented for sale.

Service, parts and accessories

Our aftersales department is equipped with Bentley factory diagnostic systems and staffed by Bentley-trained technicians. We carry genuine Bentley parts and accessories, and can arrange collection and delivery of your motor car for scheduled maintenance.

Finance and leasing

Our business managers work with Bentley Financial Services to arrange purchase and lease agreements, discreetly and on terms suited to how you intend to use your motor car.

The area we serve

West Palm Beach, Palm Beach Gardens, Jupiter, Wellington and Boca Raton, and owners throughout South Florida who ask us to collect. Sales, service and parts are reached directly on the numbers listed below.
